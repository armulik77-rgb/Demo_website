A quiet spot in a busy street

Café Aroma began because we wanted a place that served proper South Indian filter coffee without the rush. Over time we added masala chai, a few reliable snacks, and tables where people actually stay.

No loud music, no complicated menu. Just decent coffee, honest food, and space to work or talk.
